What this release protects, and where it can miss
Local rules mask supported IDs, contact details, introductory names, labeled dates of birth, passports, bank accounts, institutional IDs, labeled addresses, and terms you add. Names and free-form addresses are not comprehensively detected. PAN, UPI, IFSC, passport, and account rules check formats or labels rather than identity. Aadhaar uses a checksum. Health information remains unless you add it as a private term. Images, files, voice, and text entered directly into chatbot pages are outside protection. Obfuscated or unfamiliar secrets can be missed.
Chat insertion is a beta adapter that stops on unsupported layouts or existing drafts. Always review the draft in the chatbot before sending. AI sites can change without notice.
